{# ── Section: Sensitivity ── #}
Sensitivity

Controls how aggressively the detector separates bands from background. Lower bias = more sensitive.

Block Size 35
Neighborhood size for local thresholding. Larger values smooth out small noise.
Threshold Bias (C) 10
Offset subtracted from the local mean. Lower = more sensitive to faint bands.
{# ── Section: Morphology ── #}
Morphology

Shape operations that clean up detected regions — closing gaps and recovering faint features.

Closing Kernel 3
Fills small holes inside detected bands. Increase if bands appear fragmented.
Top-Hat Radius 25
Radius for faint band extraction. Targets bands close to background intensity.

{# ── Section: Filtering ── #}
Filtering

Size constraints to reject noise (too small) and artifacts (too large).

Min Band Area 50
Minimum pixel area. Increase to ignore small noise speckles.
Max Band Area 5000
Maximum pixel area. Decrease to reject large artifacts or smears.
